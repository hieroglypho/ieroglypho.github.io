How it works

Built on study methods that research shows work, not on reading and highlighting.

🎯 Test, don’t rereadEvery class is mostly quizzes, games and puzzles. Pulling an answer from memory strengthens it far more than reviewing it. (retrieval practice)
📅 A little, oftenYour review deck brings each sign back just before you’d forget it. Five minutes a day. (spaced repetition)
🔀 Mixed drillsDrills shuffle look-alike signs together so your eye learns the differences. (interleaving)
✍ Build it yourselfWriting and arranging signs in the ΙΕΡΟΓΛΥΦΩ editor fixes them in memory. You keep everything you make. (the drawing effect)
🏺 Real texts earlyYou read royal names in Class 1 and a genuine offering stela by the end of Module B.
📘 A standard grammarThe grammar follows James P. Allen’s Middle Egyptian (3rd ed.), the leading university textbook, and uses its transliteration. The book is optional.
